# PathVision Support — Android XR

PathVision is a path-drawing puzzle game for Android XR devices, published by Meshy on Google Play.
Contact: **davinci.dalhi@gmail.com** — we answer within 48 hours.

## Playing

PathVision opens as a window in your space. Point at a shape with your hand (or look at it, or use a controller), pinch and drag.

- **The goal:** join every pair of matching shapes. A level is solved when every pair is joined.
- **Draw:** pinch a shape and drag to its twin. Paths run along the grid, horizontally and vertically, never diagonally. Drag back over your path to shorten it; start from a shape again, or from any point of its path, to redraw it.
- **No crossing:** two paths can never share a square, and a path cannot pass over another pair's shape.
- **Stars and score:** every level has a proven shortest total route. Matching its length earns three stars (10 points), finishing within 15 % of it two stars (7 points), otherwise one star (5 points). The Length gauge shows your route against the shortest one. Your best result on each level counts.
- **Undo** takes back your last change; **Restart** clears the board. There is no timer and there are no lives, and redrawing costs nothing.
- **Stuck?** The lightbulb draws the shortest solution, one pair at a time, then clears the board so you can draw it yourself. The first time on a level it uses one solution credit; replays of that level are free. Watching it earns no stars. Pinch the lightbulb again while it plays to pause it.
- A short guided tutorial runs the first time you open the game; **How to Play → Replay Tutorial** runs it again.
- **Back** closes a window, then leaves the level.

## Worlds

- Ten worlds of 100 levels: Iron, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Emerald, Diamond, Master, Grandmaster and Legend, from four pairs on a 5×5 grid up to ten pairs on a 9×9 grid.
- The first 10 levels of Iron and level 1 of every other world are free. Each world can be unlocked with a one-time purchase, or every world at once with the All Worlds Bundle.
- Inside a world, the level after the highest one you have solved opens next.

## Purchases

- Purchases go through Google Play, with your Google account.
- If a purchase was interrupted, PathVision delivers it the next time it starts. You can also choose **Settings → Restore Purchases**, signed in to the Google account that made the purchase.
- Worlds come back on any device signed in to that account. Solution credits are part of your saved game: credits you have already used are not restored.
- Purchases and progress in PathVision on other platforms stay there. Nothing transfers between platforms.
- For a refund, see Google Play's refund policy: [support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

## Your progress

- Your progress is saved on your device. If backup is on for your Google account, Android backs it up, and it comes back when you install PathVision again; without backup, uninstalling the app deletes it.
- **Settings → Reset All Progress** clears solved levels, stars and scores. Purchases and credits stay.
