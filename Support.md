## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I play?**
A: Drag from one shape to its matching partner to draw a path between them. Paths run horizontally and vertically only, they cannot cross or overlap, and every pair must be connected to finish the level. Redrawing a path costs you nothing, so experiment freely.

**Q: How are stars awarded?**
A: Every level has a shortest possible total route, shown while you play. Match it exactly for three stars; the further over it you finish, the fewer stars you earn.

**Q: How do I sync my progress across devices?**
A: PathVision uses iCloud to sync your progress automatically. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account on all your devices and that iCloud Drive is enabled for PathVision. Progress, star ratings, solution credits, and unlocked solutions all follow you between iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro.

**Q: I lost my progress. Can I recover it?**
A: Your progress is stored in iCloud. If you reinstall the app on a device signed in to the same Apple Account, your progress should restore automatically. Sync can take a moment after a fresh install — if it doesn't appear, please contact us at the email above.

**Q: How do the worlds work?**
A: PathVision has 10 worlds — from Iron to Legend — each with 100 puzzle levels. The first two worlds, Iron and Bronze, are completely free. For every other world, level 1 is free to try, and the remaining levels are unlocked with a one-time purchase — either that single world, or every paid world at once with the all-worlds bundle.

**Q: What are solution credits?**
A: A solution credit reveals the answer to a level you're stuck on. Revealing a solution unlocks that level's solution permanently, so you can replay it as often as you like. You start with one free credit, and more can be purchased in packs.

**Q: I bought a world but it's still locked. What do I do?**
A: Open **Settings → Restore Purchases**, or the **Restore Purchases** button on any purchase screen. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account and can be restored on any device signed in to it, at no extra cost.

**Q: Does resetting my progress remove my purchases?**
A: No. **Reset All Progress** clears level completions and scores only. Purchased worlds, solution credits, and previously unlocked solutions are never revoked.

**Q: Is my data shared with third parties?**
A: No. PathVision does not collect personal information or share any data with third parties. For full details, see our [Privacy Policy]([https://meshy-sc.github.io/PathVision-Support/PrivacyPolicy](https://github.com/Meshy-SC/PathVision/blob/main/PrivacyPolicy.md)).

**Q: Does PathVision work offline?**
A: Yes. All 1,000 puzzles are bundled with the app, so you can play with no network connection. An internet connection is only needed to sync progress via iCloud and to make or restore purchases.

**Q: Is PathVision accessible?**
A: Yes. The board is fully navigable with VoiceOver — activate a shape, then activate its matching shape, and PathVision routes the path for you. The app also supports Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion, and a high-contrast token option in Settings for players who don't distinguish colors.
