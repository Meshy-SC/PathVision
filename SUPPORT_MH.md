# PathVision Support — Meta Quest

PathVision is a path-drawing puzzle game for Meta Quest, published by Meshy.
Contact: **davinci.dalhi@gmail.com** — we answer within 48 hours.

## Playing

- **The goal:** join every pair of matching shapes. A level is solved when every pair is joined.
- **Draw:** point at a shape, hold the trigger (or pinch) and drag to its twin. Paths run along the grid, horizontally and vertically, never diagonally. Drag back over your path to shorten it; start from a shape again, or from any point of its path, to redraw it.
- **No crossing:** two paths can never share a square, and a path cannot pass over another pair's shape.
- **Stars and score:** every level has a proven shortest total route. Matching its length earns three stars (10 points), finishing within 15 % of it two stars (7 points), otherwise one star (5 points). The Length gauge shows your route against the shortest one. Your best result on each level counts.
- **Undo** takes back your last change; **Restart** clears the board. There is no timer and there are no lives, and redrawing costs nothing.
- **Stuck?** The lightbulb draws the shortest solution, one pair at a time, then clears the board so you can draw it yourself. The first time on a level it uses one solution credit; replays of that level are free. Watching it earns no stars. Select the lightbulb again while it plays to pause it.
- A short guided tutorial runs the first time you open the game; **How to Play → Replay Tutorial** runs it again.
- Controllers and hands both work. Controller B or Y (Back) closes a window, then leaves the level.

## Worlds

- Ten worlds of 100 levels: Iron, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Emerald, Diamond, Master, Grandmaster and Legend, from four pairs on a 5×5 grid up to ten pairs on a 9×9 grid.
- The first 10 levels of Iron and level 1 of every other world are free. Each world can be unlocked with a one-time purchase, or every world at once with the All Worlds Bundle.
- Inside a world, the level after the highest one you have solved opens next.

## Purchases

- If a purchase was interrupted, PathVision delivers it the next time it starts. You can also choose **Settings → Restore Purchases**.
- Solution credits that you have already used are not restored.
- Purchases and progress in PathVision for Apple devices stay there. Nothing transfers between Apple devices and Meta Quest.
- For a refund, follow Meta's refund policy in the Meta Horizon Store.

## Your progress

- Your progress is saved on your headset. If cloud backup is on in your headset's settings, Meta Horizon OS backs it up, and it comes back when you install PathVision again.
- **Settings → Reset All Progress** clears solved levels, stars and scores. Purchases and credits stay.
