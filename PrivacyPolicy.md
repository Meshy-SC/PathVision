# PathVision – Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 5, 2026**

## Overview

PathVision ("the App") is a family-friendly path-drawing puzzle game designed for all ages, available on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro. We are committed to protecting your privacy — especially when it comes to young players. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

---

## Information We Collect

### 1. Game Progress Data

The App saves your game progress locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework, including:

- Worlds and levels completed
- Star ratings and scores earned per level
- Solution credits remaining, and which level solutions you have unlocked

This data is synced across your Apple devices via **iCloud / CloudKit** if you are signed in to iCloud, so your progress is preserved when switching devices. This data is stored in your own private iCloud database, is associated with your Apple Account, and is governed by Apple's iCloud privacy practices. We have no access to it.

### 2. Preferences

Your settings — sound, music, haptics, high-contrast tokens, and the chosen token skin — are stored on your device and synced with iCloud Key-Value Storage so they follow you across devices.

### 3. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The App receives only the anonymous transaction records needed to unlock what you bought. **We never see or store your name, payment details, or Apple Account credentials.**

---

## Information We Do NOT Collect

- We do **not** collect your name, email address, or any other personal information.
- We do **not** use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.
- We do **not** track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.
- We do **not** share any data with third parties.
- We do **not** collect location data.
- We do **not** access your camera, microphone, photos, or contacts.
- We do **not** display advertisements of any kind.
- We do **not** require or offer account creation.

---

## Data Storage and Security

All game data is stored locally on your device and, if iCloud is enabled, synced through Apple's secure iCloud infrastructure into your private database. We do not operate any external servers and do not have direct access to your data.

You can delete all locally stored progress at any time from **Settings → Reset All Progress**. Deleting the App removes all local data; removing PathVision's data from iCloud is done through your device's iCloud settings.

---

## In-App Purchases

PathVision offers optional one-time purchases:

- **World unlocks** — unlock all levels in a paid world (the first two worlds are free in full, and level 1 of every other world is free to try).
- **All-worlds bundle** — unlock every paid world at once.
- **Solution credit packs** — consumable credits that reveal a level's solution.

There are no subscriptions and no recurring charges. All purchases are handled by Apple; restore them at any time from **Settings → Restore Purchases**.

---

## Children's Privacy

PathVision is designed to be safe for players of all ages, including young children. The App:

- Does **not** collect any personal information from children.
- Does **not** contain advertising of any kind.
- Does **not** require account creation.
- Does **not** link to external websites within gameplay.
- Does contain optional in-app purchases, which are protected by the standard iOS parental controls and Ask to Buy. Purchase screens are presented by Apple's own StoreKit interface.

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable international children's privacy regulations. The App is rated 4+ on the App Store.

---

## Third-Party Services

The App uses the following Apple-provided frameworks, each governed by Apple's own privacy policies:

| Service | Purpose |
|---|---|
| iCloud / CloudKit | Sync game progress and purchases across your devices |
| StoreKit | Process in-app purchases |

PathVision includes no third-party SDKs whatsoever. For more information, please refer to [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Your Rights

Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, or delete on your behalf. Your game data lives on your device and in your own private iCloud storage, entirely under your control.
